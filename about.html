Who I am

Motivated, curious & growth-oriented

My name is Furqan Ali. I'm a Computer Science graduate from Islamabad, Pakistan, with a strong interest in software engineering, full-stack web development, artificial intelligence, automation and interactive digital experiences. I enjoy working out how technology works, solving practical problems and turning ideas into functional products.

I learn best by combining theory with experimentation. Rather than follow a single path, I explore different tools and workflows to see how they apply to real projects — especially where software development meets AI, and intelligent tools, automation and creative thinking build more capable, engaging products.

On the technical side I build with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, use React and Tailwind, and am learning the MERN stack. Alongside that I'm developing AI skills — AI agents, AI receptionists, chatbots and CRM workflow automation — the same services I help bring to clients as a full-stack developer at 818 AI Agency.

I know that being a strong engineer takes more than knowing languages: problem-solving, debugging, teamwork, communication, adaptability and the ability to turn incomplete ideas into maintainable solutions. Those are the skills I'm strengthening every day.
